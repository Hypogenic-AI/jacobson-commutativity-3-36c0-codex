# Downloaded Papers

This directory contains the PDF corpus gathered for the Jacobson commutativity
project on fixed exponent `x^{p^3}=x` in characteristic `p`.

Chunked versions of the highest-priority papers are in `papers/pages/`.

1. [Equational proofs of Jacobson's theorem](2310.05301_brandenburg_equational_proofs_jacobson.pdf)
   - Author: Martin Brandenburg
   - Year: 2023
   - Source: arXiv:2310.05301, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.05301
   - Why relevant: Central paper. Gives the equational framework, prime-power reductions, full proofs for `k=1` and `k=2`, and constructive Wedderburn reductions for general `k`. Contains explicit `8`- and `27`-ring examples and the main gap for `k=3`.

2. [Elementary Proofs of Ring Commutativity Theorems](2601.12599_kinyon_machale_elementary_proofs_ring_commutativity.pdf)
   - Authors: Michael K. Kinyon and Des MacHale
   - Year: 2026
   - Source: arXiv:2601.12599, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.12599
   - Why relevant: Develops elementary commutativity proofs for fixed exponents and records automated-theorem-prover support. The odd-exponent central-power lemma is directly relevant when `p` is odd.

3. [Equational proofs for a theorem by Jacobson for a significant density of even exponents](daas_equational_proofs_density_even_exponents.pdf)
   - Author: M. A. Daas
   - Year: undated student note
   - Source: https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~daasma/Eqpfs.pdf redirected to a Google Drive copy
   - Why relevant: Focuses on equational proofs for even exponents; useful context for `p=2`, where `p^3=8`.

4. [Term Rewrite Rules for Finite Fields](burris_lawrence_1991_term_rewrite_rules_finite_fields.pdf)
   - Authors: Stanley N. Burris and John Lawrence
   - Year: 1991
   - Source: https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~snburris/htdocs/MYWORKS/PAPERS/fields3.pdf
   - Why relevant: Connects finite-field identities, equational theories, and term-rewrite systems for rings satisfying identities of the form `x^m=x`.

5. [Commutativity Theorems: Examples in Search of Algorithms](wavrik_1999_commutativity_theorems_algorithms.pdf)
   - Author: John Wavrik
   - Year: 1999
   - Source: https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~jwavrik/pub/21_issac99.pdf
   - Why relevant: Describes algorithmic approaches to noncommutative polynomial reductions for commutativity theorems.

6. [A proof of Jacobson's theorem](dolan_1976_proof_of_jacobsons_theorem.pdf)
   - Author: J. M. Dolan
   - Year: 1976
   - Source: Cambridge Core PDF
   - Why relevant: Gives a short choice-free proof of Jacobson's theorem and clarifies the constructive lineage behind later equational work.

7. [On the commutativity of J-rings](luh_1967_commutativity_of_j_rings.pdf)
   - Author: Jiang Luh
   - Year: 1967
   - Source: Cambridge Core PDF
   - Why relevant: Classical structural paper for fixed-exponent `J`-rings, including `p^k`-rings and finite-field decompositions.

8. [Wedderburn's Theorem and a Theorem of Jacobson](herstein_1961_wedderburn_and_jacobson.pdf)
   - Author: I. N. Herstein
   - Year: 1961
   - Source: https://www.carolynrabbott.com/uploads/3/0/8/1/30819067/herstein-wedderburn.pdf
   - Why relevant: Historical source for elementary Wedderburn/Jacobson arguments.

9. [Variations on a theme: Rings satisfying `x^3=x` are commutative](buckley_machale_2013_variations_x3_equals_x.pdf)
   - Authors: Stephen M. Buckley and Des MacHale
   - Year: 2013
   - Source: https://archive.maths.nuim.ie/staff/sbuckley/Papers/bm_variations.pdf
   - Why relevant: Low-exponent model of elementary commutativity proofs and centrality arguments.

10. [A note on a theorem of Jacobson related to periodic rings](anderson_danchev_2020_periodic_rings_jacobson.pdf)
    - Authors: D. D. Anderson and Peter Danchev
    - Year: 2020
    - Source: AMS Proceedings PDF
    - Why relevant: Broader periodic-ring generalization of Jacobson-type commutativity.
